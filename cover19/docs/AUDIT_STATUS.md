### Geometric reduction

The proofs buffer a putative smaller radius to `r < rho < 1/sqrt(13)`, use
small generic center perturbations and ordinary restricted Voronoi cells,
and pass to an inclusion-minimal subcover at the buffered radius. The review
checked the role of private points, convex cells, the short-arc no-return
argument, and the simple disk dual. Star subdivision pads fewer than nineteen
vertices; coincident geometric coordinates are allowed in this necessary
auxiliary graph. Boundary arc and interior-cell arguments restrict the
families to `12 <= B <= 18`, `I = 19-B >= 1`.

These lemmas are not mechanically formalized. Correctness of the geometric
entrance to enumeration remains part of the mathematical trust boundary,
even though the downstream finite search was freshly replayed.

### Upper cover and degenerate triangles

The exact triangular-lattice construction has nineteen distinct centers,
twelve ordered unit-circle anchors, and twenty-four center triangles.
The covering complex has `(V,E,F) = (31,78,48)`, Euler characteristic 1,
connected face adjacency, correct vertex links, and a simple twelve-edge
boundary. Exactly six covering faces have zero area; the other forty-two
have positive oriented area.

Both proofs account for the degeneracy. A nonnegative-orientation triangle
chain whose boundary winds once around the convex anchor polygon covers
that polygon: an uncovered interior point off all face edges would have
winding number zero from every triangle, contradicting boundary winding
one. Closure covers the face-edge images. The twelve circular caps complete
the unit disk. No claim of forty-eight strictly positive Jacobians is needed.

### Different candidate lower bounds

The primary verifies a normalized positive stress on 42 rods and eliminates
24 free stressed vertices to obtain all 66 anchor conductances exactly.
An independent closed-form six-cycle elimination agrees with the star-mesh
result. On the tight convex angular domain `gap <= 2 asin(1/sqrt(13))`, an
11-dimensional rational Hessian lower bound has strictly positive LDL
pivots. A separate circulant/Fourier lower bound gives a nonconstant spectral
gap greater than `7/5000`. Exact stationarity and candidate energy `1/13`
then establish the global candidate lower bound. The additional linker
explicitly verifies that the candidate belongs to this tighter domain via
positive consecutive determinants and dot products at least `11/13`.

The alternative fixes one anchor and works with 70 physical coordinates of
the same 36-node stressed graph. Its rational matrix convention accounts
for the `sqrt(3)` scaling by congruence, not by treating rational pairs as
Euclidean coordinates. Exact checks give `gamma=400`, `lambda=1/650`,
`nu=1/156`, anchor radius `1/5`, and isolation margin `17/76050 > 0`.
The candidate tree has 263 splits, 236 strict force leaves, and 28 verified
local leaves; its entire angular domain is partitioned.

The central disk center is absent from both stressed graphs: primary index
15 corresponds to alternative index 12. Unused face witnesses are also
omitted. The full necessary graph has 96 rods; keeping 42 positive-stress
rods is a valid relaxation for a lower bound. Isolation fixes the stressed
subgraph only. Neither proof thereby classifies all optimal covers or fixes
the omitted central center in every optimal realization.

### Exact package linkage

The added [linker](../verify_bundle_linkage.py) checks all nineteen center
coordinates, all twelve anchors, all twenty-four full center faces, the six
active faces, all thirty-six stressed node coordinates, all forty-two edges
and weights, and the radial stress. The center relabeling is derived from
exact coordinate equality, not a floating-point fit. It re-executes the
primary candidate and alternative upper geometry checks in fresh copies.

It also canonicalizes every survivor in each archive with the same
breadth-first normalization, rejects duplicate classes, and compares the
entire sets and stabilizers for all seven families. The primary candidate
index is 6253; the alternative index is 6276. Both original forest verifiers
check their explicit candidate-to-topology face maps; differing indices
reflect representation order, not different constructions.

## Verification software and publication limits

The repository wrapper preserves immutable originals, deletes stale reports
only in temporary copies, requires successful subprocess exits and every
expected stage, checks exact result fields and regression totals, rejects
assertion-disabled execution, and rechecks input hashes before accepting.
Twelve synthetic wrapper regressions passed. They cover corrupt/missing/
extra inputs, unsafe paths, proof mismatch, stale reports, incomplete stage
lists, unresolved output, missing regression results, export cleanup, and
protected output paths. These wrapper tests do not replace the mathematical
checkers' 35 and 36 negative tests.

Six additional actual startup tests seeded an old successful master report
in disposable copies, then ran each original master under `-O`, under `-OO`,
and with the candidate-map input missing. Every process failed and removed
the stale master report. Results are recorded in
[`release_startup_checks.json`](../verification/release_startup_checks.json),
separately from the supplied alternative startup-test report. Repository-wide
input integrity checks for Cover11 through Cover19 also passed; these hashes
do not represent a new full replay of the older eight packages in this release.

The two archives share reductions, data, and software ancestry. Exact
agreement and different candidate arguments reduce specific linkage risks,
but do not eliminate shared mathematical or implementation errors. Trusted
components include the written geometry/topology/analysis, Python integer
and Fraction arithmetic, and the C++17 compiler/runtime. There is no Lean,
Coq, or other proof-assistant kernel check, and no claim of completed external
peer review, historical priority, or uniqueness of all optimal covers.

The release preserves the supplied proof texts and optional discovery
sources, adds reproducible verification and audit documentation, and does
not add arXiv-style PDF/LaTeX manuscripts.
