COVER12 exact verification bundle
=================================

Main replay:

  PYTHON_BIN="$(command -v python3)" bash verify_all.sh

Required software:

  Python 3.11+
  SymPy
  NumPy
  SciPy

The master verifier checks:
  - 14-variable rational Krawczyk certificate
  - symbolic contact/equilibrium/stationarity identities
  - exact threshold and trigonometric inequalities
  - exact upper covering triangulation
  - six exhaustive triangulation orbit lists and Brown counts
  - exact Farkas certificates
  - exact partition of 132 residual topologies
  - exact graph-energy certificates for 131 noncandidate residuals
  - global strict convexity certificate for the candidate topology

All final acceptance decisions use exact rational/integer arithmetic,
explicit Taylor remainder bounds, interval arithmetic, symbolic polynomial
identities, or rational LDL decomposition. Floating-point data is used only
to discover finite certificates that are then independently rechecked.

The proof is computer-assisted and has not yet undergone independent peer review.

The bundle is self-contained; exact_core.py is the shared rational/Taylor kernel.

Successful completion ends with:

  COVER12 EXACT MASTER REPLAY PASSED
